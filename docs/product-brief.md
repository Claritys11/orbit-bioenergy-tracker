# ORBIT Product Brief

## Background
ORBIT is built for JA WE Challenge 2026, theme: "Reimagine Energy: Powering Our Future from Waste." The project explores how recurring school canteen organics can become cleaner, traceable feedstock for validated community bioenergy operators.

## Problem Statement
Schools generate organic waste repeatedly, but one school may not provide enough stable feedstock for its own biodigester. Community waste volumes are larger but scattered and often contaminated. Waste management and energy planning are usually handled separately, so schools do not see clear feedback from sorting behaviour to usable energy outcomes.

## Proposed Solution
ORBIT connects schools, canteens, supporting contributors, TPS3R/KSM-style operators, and community partners in one auditable loop: waste source -> QR batch -> quality verification -> scheduled collection -> partner conversion -> verified biogas -> fair allocation -> public impact.

## Main Innovation
The Purity-to-Power Allocation Engine rewards accepted, conversion-ready feedstock instead of unverified gross waste. It separates estimated gas from verified gas and uses verified allocatable gas for allocation.

## Target Users
- Public visitors, students, parents, judges, local government, and partner candidates.
- School admins, canteen staff, students, operators, community partners, and super admins.

## Stakeholder Roles
- Schools: source-separated waste and educational feedback.
- Canteens: batch registration and pickup preparation.
- Operators: adult-managed pickup, inspection, conversion, safety, and fulfilment.
- Supporting contributors: market/vendor feedstock stabilisation.
- Community partners: allocation visibility and local benefit.

## Pilot Scope
The prototype uses fictional seed data: two schools, one operator, one supporting market/vendor, one completed conversion cycle, finalised allocation, partial fulfilment, safety examples, and simulated sensor data.

## Limitations
ORBIT does not operate biodigesters, valves, gas bags, emergency shutdown, electricity generation, Bio-CNG, PLN integration, blockchain, payments, or AI prediction. Student-facing features must not instruct students to handle hazardous equipment.

## Success Indicators
- Cleaner accepted feedstock and lower contamination.
- Verified gas recorded separately from estimates.
- Transparent allocation and fulfilment.
- Public-safe impact data with confidence labels.
- Audit trail for critical changes.
