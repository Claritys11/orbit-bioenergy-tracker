# Content Strategy

## Objectives
The public site must explain why ORBIT exists, how it works, what data can be trusted, what is still a pilot assumption, and how partners participate. The private workspace must help users complete operational tasks with plain action language.

## Core Message
Cleaner, verified, conversion-ready organic waste earns a fairer share of verified community biogas.

## CTA Hierarchy
Primary public CTA: Explore Live Impact. Secondary: See How It Works. Operational CTA: Sign In.

## Terminology
Use "registered waste", "accepted waste", "verified gas", "allocated gas", and "fulfilled gas" distinctly. Avoid raw enum labels in user-facing copy.

## Measured vs Estimated Wording
- Measured: operator inspection, verified gas records, fulfilment volume.
- Estimated: educational gas estimate, LPG equivalent, savings.
- Simulated Demo: seed dataset and sensor examples.
- Pilot Assumption: 50/30/20 allocation and 5% fee.
- Pending Field Validation: partner capabilities, route costs, legal gas-bag delivery.

## Prohibited Overclaims
Do not claim official partner validation, carbon reduction, live field results, TPS3R biodigester availability, AI optimisation, or safe gas delivery without field evidence.
